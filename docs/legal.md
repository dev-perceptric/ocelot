# Terms and Privacy: open points for legal review

`/terms` and `/privacy` are first drafts written from how Ocelot actually works today. Before the public beta, a lawyer should review them and settle these points.

| # | Point | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The legal entity that runs Ocelot, its registered address and country | Both pages say "Ocelot"; they need the real company name. It also decides the governing law |
| 2 | Governing law and where disputes are handled | Not in the Terms yet |
| 3 | Vietnam Decree 13/2023 on personal data protection: consent wording, the data protection impact assessment, and the cross-border transfer filing (data is stored in Singapore) | Most candidates are in Vietnam (check) |
| 4 | Singapore PDPA and other Southeast Asian laws for candidates and employers there | Launch market is Vietnam and Southeast Asia |
| 5 | Minimum age (16 now) | Labour and data rules for minors differ by country |
| 6 | Liability wording (section 9 of the Terms) | Plain-language placeholder |
| 7 | Paid listings: refund wording, tax and invoices | Needed before payments ship (Milestone 5) |
| 8 | Data processing terms with Supabase, Fly.io, Resend and Google | The Privacy page lists them as service providers |
| 9 | `hello@joinocelot.com` must reach a real inbox | Both pages give it as the contact. Set up forwarding in Namecheap or an inbox before launch |
| 10 | Retention periods for email logs, server logs and backups | The Privacy page says "only as long as needed"; real numbers are better |

When the pages change, update the "Last updated" date at the top of each.
